The crop

Small phones (97 × 72 tile): the sides are cut, 7 pt each. Large phones (121 × 72 tile): top and bottom are cut, 5 pt each. Where the label and the count sit: keep it free of bright detail. The art stays in the upper right, inside both crops.

Rules

  • Format. Android: WebP (quality 90) in each density bucket, 2–9 KB. iOS: an asset-catalog image set with PNG @2x and @3x (asset catalogs do not take WebP). The SVG master uses only paths, gradients and transforms, so it can be converted to a VectorDrawable or an Xcode vector asset if you prefer vector. From a server: the xxhdpi WebP.
  • Size. 144 × 96 pt (3:2). Draw it center-cropped (centerCrop / scaleAspectFill): the tile is 97–121 × 72 pt and nothing important is cut.
  • Opaque, square corners. The app rounds the tile (13), adds the bottom gradient (46 pt) and draws the label and the count.
  • No text in the image, so it works in every language.
  • Contrast. White label over the label area: at least 7.5 : 1 at every size (measured with the gradient).
